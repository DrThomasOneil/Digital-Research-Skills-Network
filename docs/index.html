Digital Research Skills Network


Welcome!

Welcome to Digital Research Skills Network! Whether you’re just starting out with R or looking to strengthen your skills, this site is designed to foster a digital community centered around biomedical research programmatic analysis.

Bookmark this site for quick access to upcoming events, past event recordings and resources.

May 13th

AI in Research Town Hall (11 - 12 pm)

The session will begin with a short presentation from Harry Robertson on the current landscape of generative AI, including emerging opportunities, limitations and local govts position on the topic. This will be followed by a town hall-style discussion with guided prompts to explore how different groups are currently using AI, where they see value or risks, and what should be considered in the development of this new policy.

Zoom ID: 82210822641

Interfacing with the NCI HPC for imaging analysis (1 - 3 pm)

Join us on 13th May for a demonstration on accessing and navigating a HPC interface. Sifan Zhang will demonstrate Cellpose image segmentation and Alexandra Carey Hoppe will demonstrate Interacting with imaging software like QuPath. This guide has been developed to support researchers in setting up their computational workflows on NCI’s Gadi HPC.

Zoom ID: 84164373602
